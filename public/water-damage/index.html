Understanding the Numbers

What RCV, O&P, depreciation, and ACV mean, and how they relate

RCV Replacement Cost Value
What it costs today to actually complete the repair — current material prices plus labor — with no deduction for the age or condition of what's being replaced.
O&P Overhead & Profit
A 20% markup (10% overhead + 10% profit) built into each line item to cover a general contractor's cost of running the job — scheduling, permits, supervising subs — plus their profit margin.
Deprec. Depreciation
A reduction applied to older or worn materials, like the existing paint, to reflect their age and condition right before the loss. Newer materials depreciate less.
ACV Actual Cash Value
What the damaged property was actually worth right before the loss. This is the amount typically paid out up front.

RCV − Depreciation = ACV. Because this policy has replacement-cost coverage, that depreciation isn't lost — it's "recoverable." Once repairs are complete and proof is submitted, it gets paid out too, which is where the "Net Claim if Recovered" figure above comes from.
