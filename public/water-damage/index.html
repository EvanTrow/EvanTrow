Water Damage Repair - Scope of Work

Exterior siding/electrical repair, interior drywall & paint, and garage ceiling repair

Exterior of House

  1. Set up staging and scaffolding on and over stairs on side of house
  2. Remove siding above light down to the bottom of house
  3. Mark and label all siding (so it can be reinstalled)
  4. Remove house wrap in affected areas
  5. Cut out and remove house sheeting as needed
  6. Install new electrical box for exterior light
  7. Repair bottom plate of exterior as needed
  8. Install new sheeting in repaired areas
  9. Remove band board and replace as needed
  10. Install new house wrap as needed
  11. Tape and seal all new house wrap
  12. Remove and tear down staging for homeowner's access to house
  13. Re-set up staging to finish house repairs
  14. Install new mount master for exterior light
  15. Install rubber flashing tape around light box to help stop water
  16. Caulk and seal electrical box into house
  17. Reinstall old siding onto house

Living Room

  1. Install new insulation in wall
  2. Install new drywall patch in living room
  3. Finish drywall patch in living room
  4. Install temporary plastic walls to control dust from sanding drywall
  5. Sand drywall and clean up mess
  6. Paint entire wall floor to ceiling, corner to corner
  7. Install new trim on wall
  8. Paint trim on affected area

Garage

  1. Replace insulation in affected area of ceiling
  2. Install 5/8" drywall patch in ceiling
  3. Finish drywall patch in ceiling
  4. Paint ceiling on right side of garage

Painting

  1. All patches will have one coat of primer
  2. Affected area will get 2 coats of paint for proper blending
